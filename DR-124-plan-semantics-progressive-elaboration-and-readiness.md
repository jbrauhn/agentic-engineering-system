# DR-124 — Plan Semantics, Progressive Elaboration, and Execution Readiness

**Status:** Adopted  
**Date:** 2026-08-22

## Decision

Canonical AE Plans carry the minimum provider-neutral semantics required to connect an exact approved Contract and Product/System Baseline to reviewed, architecture-aware, executable work and a credible Verification/evidence route.

Rolling-wave Planning is valid. Deferred detail may be elaborated without a new Plan revision only inside an explicitly reviewed elaboration envelope. Material change to reviewed scope, dependency/barrier topology, architecture assumptions, authority/capability assumptions, Verification/evidence route, risk/constraint assumptions, sequencing, or adaptation boundaries requires a new Plan revision and applicable review.

Execution readiness is an AE governance determination based on exact/effective governing revisions, scoped Plan Review, dependency/barrier resolution, Context Requirements, Capability Bindings, authority, technical prerequisites, Verification/evidence expectations, adaptation boundaries, and absence of blocking conditions. Provider workflow labels do not establish AE readiness.

## Rationale

This preserves rolling-wave speed without allowing deferred detail or provider status to bypass exact-revision governance.

## Consequences

- reviewed scope may execute while future scope remains in Planning;
- unaffected scope may continue under an older reviewed Plan revision only after deterministic impact analysis;
- changed scope never silently inherits prior review;
- one Plan may have multiple representations without becoming multiple Plan entities;
- no universal Planning Method or giant Plan form is required.
