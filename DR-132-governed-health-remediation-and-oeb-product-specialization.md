# DR-132 — Governed health remediation and OEB/Product specialization

**Status:** Adopted  
**Date:** 2026-08-22

## Decision

Standards gaps and Engineering Health Findings route through the existing Canonical AE lifecycle rather than a separate remediation workflow.

Depending on effect, the governed response may be:

- local adaptation inside reviewed boundaries;
- Replan;
- Contract Change Proposal/G5;
- Escalate;
- explicit deferral or accepted risk where authorized;
- an existing/new remediation AE Loop/Contract.

Remediation outcome is judged through normal L1-I independent Validation. The discovering/implementing path may create Evidence but cannot self-validate remediation success.

OEB coordinates organization standards sources/defaults, Evidence/Validation expectations, exception authority/process references, and known health state. Product/System Profile may tighten/narrow/specialize/add product requirements but may not silently weaken mandatory higher constraints.

Effective standards/health configuration and current-health views remain derived projections rather than new A1 entities.

An organization does not need pristine engineering health before adopting AE. Material weaknesses are governed and can become agent-assisted improvement work.

## Rationale

This fulfills Contract §§9–10 and Adoption Proof K–L while preserving one lifecycle, proportional adoption, exact/effective state, and non-destructive history.

## Rejected alternatives

- Separate health-remediation lifecycle: duplicates Contract/Planning/Execution/Validation semantics.
- Remediation-before-adoption for every finding: contradicts the Contract's imperfect-organization adoption intent.
- Product last-writer-wins standards configuration: could silently weaken mandatory organization constraints.
- Assessor self-validation of remediation: violates L1-I independence.
